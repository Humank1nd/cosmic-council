#### 🟢 Green Tortoise (Budget and Resources)

- **Systems Thinking Role**: Ensures the viability and sustainability of solutions across all systems
- **Interconnections**: Evaluates and optimizes resource allocation for all other segments' outputs

#### 🔵 Blue Dolphin (Communication and Marketing)

- **Systems Thinking Role**: Facilitates information flow and stakeholder engagement across all systems
- **Interconnections**: Translates insights from all segments into clear communication for stakeholders

- **Interconnections**: Monitors and provides feedback on the effectiveness of all other segments

### 2. Cyclical Synergy

The segments interact in a cyclical manner, symbolized by the perpetual motion of the White Rabbit and Rainbow Snake:

#### White Rabbit (Time and Cycles)
- **Symbolism**: Represents the temporal dimension of systems thinking
- **Function**: Moves through six phases of the cycle:
  1. **Emergence**: New possibilities and ideas arise
  2. **Exploration**: Ideas are explored and developed
  3. **Connection**: Ideas connect with existing systems
  4. **Flow**: Information and energy flow between systems
  5. **Synthesis**: Different elements combine to create new understanding
  6. **Transformation**: Systems evolve and transform

#### Rainbow Snake (Interconnectedness and Flow)
- **Symbolism**: Represents the spatial dimension of systems thinking
- **Function**: Creates flowing connections between all six segments:
  - Red → Orange: Research informs planning
  - Orange → Yellow: Planning guides development
  - Yellow → Green: Development requires resource allocation
  - Green → Blue: Resources enable communication
  - Blue → Purple: Communication facilitates support
  - Purple → Red: Support feedback informs new research
