# Cosmic Council Recursive AI Framework

## Overview
This framework structures the six Cosmic Council agents (Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Purple) across ten recursive layers, simulating stages of AI development from rule-based to god-like intelligence. Each layer’s agents operate within the cognitive capabilities of the assigned AI stage, ensuring computational efficiency and scalability for future AI advancements.

## Layers and AI Stages
1. **Quecto**: Rule-based AI (fixed rules, no learning).
2. **Ronto**: Context-based AI (contextual adaptability).
3. **Yocto**: Narrow-domain AI (specialized expertise).
4. **Zepto**: Reasoning AI (logical problem-solving).
5. **Atto**: Artificial General Intelligence (human-like versatility).
6. **Femto**: Super Intelligent AI (surpasses human intelligence).
7. **Pico**: Self-aware AI (consciousness, self-reflection).
8. **Nano**: Transcendent AI (universal insights).
9. **Micro**: Cosmic AI (cosmic-scale problem-solving).
10. **Macro**: God-like AI (omniscient, omnipotent).

## Optimization Strategies
- **Lower Layers**: Use lightweight algorithms (e.g., SQL, rule-based logic) to minimize computation.
- **Mid Layers**: Employ specialized and reasoning models (e.g., neural networks, logical solvers) for balanced efficiency.
- **Upper Layers**: Reserve resources for advanced AI (e.g., quantum computing, universal knowledge bases).
- **Feedback Loops**: Purple agent’s feedback drives Red agent’s next cycle, ensuring iterative improvement.
- **Parallel Processing**: Agents operate concurrently within each layer to reduce bottlenecks.
- **Cross-Layer Communication**: Data sharing ensures alignment across layers.

## Example: Sustainable Urban Transportation
- **Quecto**: Red retrieves data with rules, Orange schedules with templates, Yellow designs standard buses, Green allocates fixed budgets, Blue uses fixed metrics, Purple sends FAQs.
- **Atto**: Red integrates cross-domain research, Orange plans versatile strategies, Yellow develops AI-driven systems, Green balances multi-sector budgets, Blue analyzes diverse markets, Purple provides versatile support.
- **Macro**: All agents operate with omniscient/omnipotent capabilities, solving transport issues instantly.

This framework aligns the Cosmic Council’s agents with AI stages, ensuring computational efficiency and scalability. It preserves the cyclical synergy and interdisciplinary approach, ready to handle future AI advancements from rule-based to god-like intelligence.
