Instruction Guide

The Cosmic Council operates as a cyclical problem-solving framework. Below are the step-by-step instructions for how to use the Council effectively for any project, challenge, or idea:

1. Define Your Problem or Goal

🔴 Start with clarity:

  • What is the challenge, idea, or question you want to explore?
  • Is it a problem to solve, a project to execute, or a concept to refine?Example: "How can we launch a sustainable product in a competitive market?"

Output: A clearly articulated goal or problem statement.

2. Engage All Six Members of the Council

Each member (triangle) has a unique role. Allow the Council to work cyclically, ensuring every step informs the next. Use their guiding questions to structure your process:

🔴 1. Red Owl (Inquiry & Research)

  • Purpose: Gather foundational knowledge and ask critical questions.
  • Actions:
    • Research the problem’s history, context, and causes.
    • Identify key data, trends, and existing insights.
    • Frame the "root cause" questions to explore further.
  • Guiding Question: What do we need to know, and where should we look to find it?

Output: A comprehensive knowledge base.

🟠 2. Orange Orangutan (Planning & Logistics)

  • Purpose: Organize and structure a plan based on insights from Red Owl.
  • Actions:
    • Create a step-by-step roadmap or action plan.
    • Prioritize tasks, resources, and timelines.
    • Identify obstacles and dependencies.
  • Guiding Question: How do we move forward efficiently and strategically?

Output: A detailed and actionable plan.

🟡 3. Yellow Honeybee (Development & Creativity)

  • Purpose: Develop creative solutions, prototypes, and innovations.
  • Actions:
    • Brainstorm novel approaches to solve the problem.
    • Create prototypes, models, or experiments.
    • Iterate on solutions, exploring multiple possibilities (superposition).
  • Guiding Question: What can we create or innovate to address this challenge?

Output: A prototype, innovation, or creative concept.

🟢 4. Green Turtle (Budgeting & Resources)

  • Purpose: Allocate resources and assess feasibility.
  • Actions:
    • Determine time, budget, and energy requirements.
    • Assess the sustainability and scalability of the solution.
    • Optimize for efficiency and minimal waste.
  • Guiding Question: How can we use our resources wisely and sustainably?

Output: A resource allocation plan or feasibility report.

🔵 5. Blue Dolphin (Communication & Marketing)

  • Purpose: Share the solution with stakeholders and audiences.
  • Actions:
    • Craft messaging, branding, and narratives for outreach.
    • Plan communication strategies (social media, presentations, etc.).
    • Consider target audiences and key stakeholders.
  • Guiding Question: How can we share this solution effectively with the world?

Output: A communication or marketing strategy.

🟣 6. Purple Elephant (Reflection & Empathy)

  • Purpose: Reflect on the outcomes, collect feedback, and refine the solution.
  • Actions:
    • Assess the emotional and ethical impact of the solution.
    • Gather feedback from stakeholders and users.
    • Refine the solution for continuous improvement.
  • Guiding Question: What have we learned, and how can we improve further?

Output: Feedback analysis and next steps.

3. Utilize the Council’s Cyclical Process

The Council is designed to operate in a continuous loop, ensuring that each stage informs and enhances the next:

  1. Use insights from Purple Elephant to refine the Red Owl’s research.
  1. Adjust Orange Orangutan’s plans based on real-world feedback.
  1. Expand Yellow Honeybee’s creative ideas with new perspectives.

Example: If a prototype developed by Yellow Honeybee receives negative feedback during the Purple Elephant stage, the insights will guide Red Owl to research a better approach, creating a loop of constant refinement.

4. Automate and Track Progress

  • Use Airtable, Make.com, or similar tools to:
    • Log each phase of the Council’s work.
    • Track feedback, outcomes, and iterations.
    • Visualize the process through a hexagonal workflow.
  • Example: Create a table with fields like "Stage," "Date," "Output," and "Next Steps."

5. Leverage AI Collaboration

If working with AI tools (like GPT-based models), use the Council’s framework to:

  • Prompt the AI to simulate different Council members:
    • E.g., “As Red Owl, analyze the root cause of this issue.”
  • Generate creative content or solutions using Yellow Honeybee’s energy.
  • Request empathetic feedback through Purple Elephant’s lens.

This ensures the AI thinks holistically, respecting both logical and emotional dimensions of problem-solving.

6. Apply Across Fields

The Cosmic Council’s framework is highly adaptable and can be applied to:

  • Business: Strategic planning, resource management, and marketing.
  • AI Development: Training bias-free models, ethical frameworks.
  • Personal Growth: Creating habits, achieving goals.
  • Creative Projects: Game design, writing, art.
  • Global Challenges: Climate action, education, governance.

7. Evolve with Feedback

The Cosmic Council thrives on continuous improvement:

  • After completing the first cycle, reflect deeply on the feedback.
  • Loop back to Red Owl to incorporate new questions and insights.

Example Workflow

Challenge: Launching a sustainable relaxation YouTube channel.

  1. 🔴 Red Owl: Research relaxation video trends and audience preferences.
  1. 🟠 Orange Orangutan: Plan content themes, upload schedules, and division of tasks.
  1. 🟡 Yellow Honeybee: Create pilot videos (e.g., ASMR or nature scenes).
  1. 🟢 Green Turtle: Budget time, equipment, and editing costs.
  1. 🔵 Blue Dolphin: Promote the channel via social media campaigns.
  1. 🟣 Purple Elephant: Gather viewer feedback and adjust content strategy.

Guiding Principles

  1. Balance logic and intuition: Combine data-driven insights with creativity and empathy.
  1. Think holistically: Consider interconnected factors—resources, communication, and ethics.
  1. Refine continuously: Treat each cycle as a step toward greater clarity and innovation.

By following these steps, the Cosmic Council becomes a powerful tool for problem-solving, growth, and innovation, adaptable to any field or challenge. Would you like help applying this process to a specific project? 🌟
